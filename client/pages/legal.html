Shipping, Privacy & Terms

Delivery information and the policies that apply when you shop on AgroShield.

🚚 Shipping Information

  • Delivery timeline: orders are typically delivered in 2–5 business days within serviceable PIN codes.
  • Delivery charges: a flat ₹49 applies to orders below ₹499; orders of ₹499 and above ship free.
  • Order tracking: every order receives an order ID (for example AS-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX) that can be tracked from My Account → My Orders. Statuses progress through Placed → Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered.
  • Cancellations: orders can be cancelled before they are dispatched; the stock is returned to inventory automatically.
  • Demonstration store: this is a demo project — no physical goods are shipped and no real payments are collected (Cash on Delivery and a clearly-labelled demo payment option are simulated).

🔒 Privacy Policy

AgroShield collects only the information needed to run the store:

  • Account details — name, email, phone number and delivery address, provided by you at registration and used for login, order fulfilment and support.
  • Order history — products ordered, quantities, prices and delivery addresses, retained to provide order tracking and cancellations.
  • Session token — after login a signed authentication token is stored in a secure, httpOnly cookie so you stay signed in. Logging out removes it.
  • Shopping cart — cart contents are kept in your own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to the server until you place an order.

We do not sell your data. This demo store signs you in with your registered email address only — it does not collect or store a password for new accounts. You can request deletion of your account data by contacting support@agroshield.example.

📜 Terms of Service

  • By registering you confirm the details you provide are accurate and that you will keep your login credentials secure.
  • Product availability and prices are confirmed at the time an order is placed; the store re-verifies stock and pricing on the server before an order is accepted.
  • Seed germination figures, pesticide label/registration content and manufacturer details shown in this project are clearly labelled demo data and must not be relied upon for real agricultural decisions.
  • Sale of pesticides and seeds in India is subject to the Insecticides Act, 1968 and applicable state licensing. A real deployment requires valid registrations, licences and verified product labels.
  • Any misuse of the platform — including fraudulent orders or attempts to access administrator areas — will result in account termination.
