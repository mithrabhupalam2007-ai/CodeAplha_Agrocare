Safe farming

Read the label. Protect yourself. Protect the crop.

Every crop-protection product page on AgroShield shows composition, label instructions, safety precautions, storage and disposal guidance — clearly marked as demo data during development.

  • Always follow the original product label and local regulations.
  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while spraying.
  • Store chemicals locked, away from food and children.
  • Never burn or bury empty containers — follow local disposal rules.

Need help?

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order on AgroShield?

Create a free account, add products to your cart, open the cart and proceed to checkout. Fill in your delivery details, choose Cash on Delivery or the demo payment option, and confirm. You can track everything under "My Orders".

What are the delivery charges?

Orders of ₹499 and above get free delivery. Below ₹499 a flat ₹49 delivery charge applies. The final amount is always recalculated on the server when you place the order.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is supported, along with a clearly labelled demo payment option used for presentations. This demo site never collects real card or UPI details.

How do I know seed quality and germination rates?

Each seed product page lists variety, crop type, pack size, germination information and sowing instructions wherever available. On this demo store all seed data is clearly marked as demonstration content.

Are the pesticide products real?

No. All pesticide listings are clearly labelled demo data built for development and internship demonstration. Before any real sale, applicable Indian pesticide registration, seller licensing and accurate label information must be verified and displayed.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes — orders in "Placed" or "Confirmed" status can be cancelled from the My Orders page. Stock is automatically returned to inventory when a cancellation happens.
